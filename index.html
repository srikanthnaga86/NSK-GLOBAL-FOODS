FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions.

Quick answers to help you understand our process and offerings.

❓ What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For commercial buyers, the MOQ is 500kg per grade. First-time trial orders can start at 50kg. Bulk orders (>2 MT) qualify for discount pricing.

❓ What payment methods do you accept?

We prefer Letter of Credit (L/C). We also accept Bank Transfers (TT) with 30% advance payment and balance on shipment. Bank details provided upon inquiry.

❓ How long does shipping take?

Processing: 7-10 days. Shipping times: GCC 10-15 days, USA 20-25 days, Europe 25-30 days. Faster expedited shipping available upon request.

❓ Are your cashews certified organic?

Our cashews are Non-GMO certified and ISO 22000 food-safe. Organic certification available upon request. All products are lab-tested and contaminant-free.

❓ What is your shelf life guarantee?

12 months in sealed containers at room temperature (15-25°C). We also provide vacuum-sealed and moisture-barrier packaging for extended shelf life.

❓ Can you provide custom packaging?

Yes! We offer custom branding, packaging sizes, and labeling for retail partners. Bulk options available: 25kg, 50kg bags, or full container loads.
